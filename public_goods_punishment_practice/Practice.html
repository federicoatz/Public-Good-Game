Practice: public good with punishment

This page lets you practice a version of the public goods game where, after contributing, each participant can pay to reduce the earnings of one or more group members. The other three members' contributions are generated by the system, so you can focus on the game's mechanics.

4 players per group Endowment: {{ C.ENDOWMENT }} MU Efficiency factor: {{ C.EFFICIENCY_FACTOR }} 5-minute timeout

Step 1: contribution

You have {{ C.ENDOWMENT }} Monetary Units (MU). Once all four group members have decided how much to contribute, the group fund will contain the sum of all contributions. Each member will receive {{ C.EFFICIENCY_FACTOR }} MU from the group fund for every MU it contains.

Clicking "Submit" will make the system generate the other group members' contributions.

For practice purposes, the other group members' contributions are generated by the system.
